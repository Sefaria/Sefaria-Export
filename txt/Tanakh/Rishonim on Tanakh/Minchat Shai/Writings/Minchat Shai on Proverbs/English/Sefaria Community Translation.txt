Verse 16

<b>All the judges of righteousness</b> — Many scribes and scholars have been doubtful about this word since years of yore, whether it is meant to be ארץ or צדק, and they did not know how to determine. There are ancient variants where first was written צדק and after that it was scraped off and ארץ was written, and there are variants were the opposite happened, and there are some where it's "mounted atop two steeds" per se, writing ארץ צדק, and there are some where they wrote one word in the inner part and the other word in the outer part, and I don't know what I'd decide for it, lacking the understanding to repair the breach or judge rightly. That being said, I found the Targum says "judges of תריצותא," and I also saw that the Meir Nativ brings that with the root "nobility."
