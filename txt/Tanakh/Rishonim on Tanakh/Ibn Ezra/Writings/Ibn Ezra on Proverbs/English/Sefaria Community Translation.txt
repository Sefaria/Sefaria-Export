Verse 1

<b>The words of Agur —</b> he was in the days of Solomon, walking in righteousness, knowing knowledge, and honored in his generation, so the king Solomon gathered the words of his wisdom into his book.
<b>son of —</b> <i>bin</i> with a <i>ḥiriq</i> is equivalent to <i>ben</i> with a <i>tsere</i>. See also <i>rish</i> (Prov. 28:19) and <i>resh</i> (Prov. 13:18), <i>bin Nun</i> (Ex. 33:11 <i>et al.</i>), <i>bin layla</i> (Jon. 4:10), <i>bin hakot</i> (Deut. 25:2).
<b>the burden —</b> the prophecy, as a matter the Name bears upon a human.
<b>the saying of —</b><i>ne'um</i> is in the same noun form as <i>zevul</i>.
<b>the man —</b> (<i>ha-gever</i>), that is, Agur, since his wisdom was great (<i>gavra</i>).
<b>Itiel and Uchal</b> — were sages, fellows or students of Agur.
Another interpretation: <b>son of Yaqeh —</b> a son of peoples' obedience (<i>yiqhat 'amim</i>). For regarding David it was said of his son, Shlomo, son of Yaqeh, and Qohelet, and Agur — that is, <b>the saying of the man to Itiel</b> — about the matter of women and horses (Deuteronomy 17:16-17), as he said: "I will increase, but my heart will not stray, and I am capable,"<sup>1</sup><i class="footnote">Punning on the names Itiel = God is with me, and Ukhal = I am capable</i> which was foolish. Itiel repented after that and said, "I acted as one who had not learned wisdom nor known heavenly knowledge." And Qohelet sought knowledge in judgment to acquit and convict without witnesses, but the Torah says only with witnesses. And about this it is said <b>Qohelet sought</b> (Eccl. 12:10) — i.e. that he be answered from heaven, but it was not so. Or the verse says <b>upright words of truth</b> (<i>ibid.</i>) for the Torah: <b>every word of God is forged</b>, <b>do not add</b> etc. (Prov. 30:5-6). "But I did not ask the right thing, for I asked what was not mine to ask." What was he compared to Moses who <b>went up to heaven and went down</b> (Prov. 30:4), or Aaron who <b>gathered the winds</b> (<i>ibid.</i>), as it is said, <b>and he stood between the living and the dead</b> (Num. 17:13), or Elijah who <b>bound the water with a cloak</b>, or Joshua who <b>established all the earth's ceasings</b> — <b>Sun be still in Gibeon</b> (Joshua 10:12)!?

Verse 4

<b>Who is it who ascended to heaven and descended? —</b> that is to say, that he has power to go up to the high heavens and descend to earth which is of a depth I do not know with all my wisdom.
<b>With his palms —</b> that the listener understand that his palms and his hands are his will or his cunning, that is to say that he would gather the wind that it not blow out.
<b>Who bundled the waters —</b> as if he bundled them in clouds, which are like a cloak, to rain down on the land.
<b>All the earth's ceasings —</b> that they not fail, and the ceasings are the ends which stand upon the void.
<b>What is his name —</b> for their sons did not reach their name and their knowledge, even I, as I am one of them! and now I ask two things of you.
<b>What is his name and what is his son's name —</b> that he know it in his wisdom.
<b>For you know —</b> you, Itiel, a man or his son, tell me one name of theirs that you know. And the meaning: he cannot know it, and no creations know His wisdom.

Verse 5

<b>Every word of God is forged —</b> meaning, the words of the LORD are pure words, and they are the commandments forged like pure gold, and among them are wisdom of the fear of the Name, and it is not worth it to engage in anything else. Thus <b>do not add to its words</b> (Prov. 30:6), you, Itiel, i.e. <b>do not add to it nor remove from it</b> (Deut. 13:1).
<b>A shield —</b> the initial <i>mem</i> in <i>magen</i> is not part of the root, as in <i>menagen<i> (musician), <i>mishberei el</i> (divine waves).</i></i>
<b>God, do not, two</b> — all three verses are connected, in the manner of prayer.

Verse 10

"אשת חיל מי ימצא (Who can find a woman of worth):" "חיל" (worth) means wealth, as in a woman who acquires wealth and possessions through her wisdom. Who can find such a woman?
"מכרה (her price):" Derived from "תמכרו" (you will sell) from "תמכרו מאתם" (you will sell from among you).
