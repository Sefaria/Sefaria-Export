Verse 5

ON THE OTHER SIDE OF THE JORDAN, IN THE LAND OF MOAB, MOSES UNDERTOOK TO EXPOUND.  Since they were no longer wandering, as the steppes of Moab was the camping place after the last leg of the journey.  Besides, after Moshe had pled his case and despaired of being able to cross, he undertook to clarify matters as to which he thought uncertainty might arise after his death.  He began his clarification by saying, later on (Deut. 5:2), "Adonai our God made a covenant with us at Horeb." AND HE SAID.  And before he began the clarification, in which he multiplied his admonitions regarding some of the mitzvot, he said to Israel that what necessitated this clarification and the admonitions now was that they were crossing into the Land without him, and he would not be able to admonish them when the need arose, nor to clarify any point of uncertainty that might arise.  And he recounted how they had failed in that he could not cross with them, through their poor choice and their sins, so that they would avoid this from here on and not ruin their affairs.

Verse 24

YOUR MIGHTY HAND.  To alter the nature of the unchanging elements of the created world, which no one but You can do. You performed all of these deeds to bring Israel to the land, and therefore it is appropriate to secure their settlement there.

Verse 25

(1) LET ME CROSS OVER. To eliminate all the residents of Canaan, so that Israel may never be exiled from it. 
(2) AND LET ME SEE THE GOOD LAND. Let me set my eyes on it as a positive force in my blessing, so that it will be good for Israel forever.

Verse 26

ADONAI BECAME ANGRY WITH ME FOR YOUR SAKE [JPS: ON YOUR ACCOUNT].  Because I yearned to establish you in it so that you would never be exiled from it, but He had already sworn to scatter them.

Verse 2

DO NOT TAKE ANYTHING AWAY FROM IT, SO AS TO SAFEGUARD.  No person should think that, since the concerns underlying the mitzvah are no longer present for that person, eliminating the mitzvah would not be a violation, as King Solomon thought: "I will multiply [wives] and not go astray; I will multiply [horses] and not send back [to Egypt]," and he succumbed.

Verse 3

YOU HAVE SEEN WITH YOUR OWN EYES.  Look, evidence of this [of lesser violations leading to greater ones] is what you saw happen in connection with Ba'al Pe'or.  For indeed, those who sinned in worshiping Ba'al Pe'or did not initially intend to do that; rather, their initial intent was solely to fornicate, as it says (Num. 25:1), "the people began by whoring."  And even though the Torah forbade this out of concern [lest it lead to] idolatry, as they said (Ex. 34:16), "their daughters will lust [after their gods] and will cause your sons to lust [after their gods]."  Each one of them thought that this would not happen to him at all, and the opposite happened -- each one who went after Ba'al Pe'or to cleave to their daughters, there was not one of them who wisely refrained from succumbing to [idolatrous] worship itself, with the result that ADONAI YOUR GOD DESTROYED HIM.

Verse 4

BUT YOU WHO HELD FAST TO ADONAI YOUR GOD ARE ALL ALIVE TODAY.  You were all  wise enough to refrain from stumbling into the worship of Ba'al Peor.

Verse 12

(1) AND IT WILL BE BECAUSE YOU HEARKEN.  The King commanded all of these so that you will merit that He keep the covenant and the kindness.  And by your paying attention today to do them out of love, and not in order to receive a reward, so He, for the sake of this, will maintain the covenant and the lovingkindness for you. 
(2) THE RULES (MISHPATIM).  For (Prov. 29:4) "by justice (mishpat) [the king] sustains the land" [as applied here, it is primarily through observance of civil law that God will set out the Land for us].
(3) AND YOU WILL TAKE CARE.  This is study (e.g., Sifrei 58).  
(4) TO DO THEM.  And in this manner, you will do them in the proper way.
(5) THEN GOD WILL KEEP FOR YOU THE COVENANT that He swore, saying (Gen. 17:7), "I will maintain My covenant between Me and you, and your offspring to come, as an everlasting covenant throughout the ages, to be God to you and to your offspring to come."  For indeed, in His being God to us, without any intermediary, there will flow down permanent existence from Him without any intermediary, as it says (Eccl. 3:14), "whatever God has brought to pass will recur evermore:"  However, the existence of the transient ones is surely from Him but mediated.
(6) AND THE KINDNESS.  As it says (Gen. 17:8), "I assign the land you sojourn in to you and your offspring to come."  For surely, the good that inures in this world to the righteous is kindness (hesed) alone, and not compensation for their good deeds.

Verse 13

(1) [GOD] WILL LOVE YOU.  By virtue of your being children, in His likeness and His image, as [the Sages] said (Avot 3:14), "Beloved are [Israel] in that they are called children to the Omnipresent," and in this way the eternal nature of the covenant will be a happy one.  
(2) AND HE WILL BLESS YOU.  With wealth for the transitory life, and in this way, He will provide the hesed as when he swore that you would live on the land happily.

Verse 14



Verse 15

(1) ALL SICKNESS.  Even those that customarily arise naturally.  
(2) DREADFUL. Contagious.  
(3) ABOUT WHICH YOU KNOW.  That [the Egyptians] were smitten with at the Sea, as is explained above [on Exod. 14:24].  
(4) HE WILL NOT BRING THEM UPON YOU BUT WILL INFLICT THEM UPON ALL YOUR ENEMIES.  Even though He will inflict them on your ememies, they will not infect you, as in (Ps. 91:7), "A thousand may fall at your left side ... but it shall not reach you."

Verse 16

(1) DO NOT SHOW THEM PITY AND DO NOT WORSHIP THEIR GODS.  Do not pity, and in this way you will not worship their gods, for if you pity, they will certainly cause you to sin.  
(2) FOR THAT WOULD BE A SNARE TO YOU.  For certainly your pitying them will be a snare for you, as they will seduce you to worship their gods.

Verse 1

(1) YOU ARE CHILDREN OF ADONAI YOUR GOD. YOU SHALL NOT GASH YOURSELVES. It is not appropriate to display the extreme of worry and pain for a dead relative when there remains a more esteemed relative. Thus you are children of Adonai, for He is your Father, enduring forever, and it is not appropriate for you to worry and mourn in the extreme for any death. ...
(2) NOR SHALL YOU SHAVE THE FRONT OF YOUR HEADS BECAUSE OF THE DEAD, FOR YOU ARE A PEOPLE CONSECRATED. Nor should one be very sorrowful for the injury experienced by the dead person in his death, for you are a holy people, destined for the life in the world to come, and the satisfaction in that life is better than all the life in this world.

(1) MAGISTRATES AND OFFICIALS.  After the mitzvot for the multitudes, he commanded matters for their leaders, they being the judges [16:18 - 17:1], the kings [17:14-20], the priests [18:1-8], and the prophets [18:14-22], in whose perfection there would be perfected the affairs of the multitudes, and in whose corruption those affairs would be corrupted, as the prophet testified, in saying (Tz'fania 3:3-4), "The officials within her / Are roaring lions; / Her judges are wolves of the steppe, / They leave no bone until morning. / Her prophets are reckless, / Totally faithless; / Her priests profane what is holy, / They give perverse rulings." 
(2) IS GIVING YOU FOR YOUR TRIBES.  In your gates that He is giving you for you to apportion among the tribes, not in what you may conquer outside the Land, which will not be apportioned among the tribes, as they said (see Makkot 7a), Outside the Land, you do not establish judges in every district and every city.  And this is as in Syria and the like.  
(3) DUE JUSTICE.  Let the receiving of the complaints be such that they will render verdicts justly, and not be lenient on this one and harsh on that one.

Verse 20

(1) JUSTICE, JUSTICE SHALL YOU PURSUE. (2) SO THAT YOU MAY LIVE AND OCCUPY.  You will need this more in the Land, because the lack of it will interfere with the occupying of the Land, as he said (Isa. 57:17), "For their sinful greed I was angry."

Verse 2

SOME OF THE BEST OF ALL THE FRUITS [JPS:  SOME OF EVERY FIRST FRUIT] OF THE SOIL WHICH YOU HARVEST FROM THE LAND.  The choicest of all its fruits, as in (Amos 6:6), "they anoint themselves with the choicest [reishit] oils," [and] (Amos 6:1), "notables of the leading [reishit] nations."  The "choicest" are the seven species with which the Land of Israel is distinguished, as we have from tradition.  This is the explanation of what is commanded above when it says (Ex. 23:19), "The choice first fruits of your soil you shall bring."  And it makes clear that in saying "The choice first fruits of your soil," the intent was that you should bring the first fruits from the choicest fruits of your soil, and those are the seven species with which it is distinguished.  

Verse 3

(1) TO THE PRIEST WHO WILL  BE IN THOSE DAYS.  Even if he is not very wise, do not desist from speaking with him respectfully, saying "Adonai your God," even though this is said only to persons of renown, as to kings and to prophets.  Nevertheless, when you bring him the first fruits, like bringing a tribute to God, may He be exalted, for He is the owner of the soil, it is appropriate to speak with him respectfully, 
(2) I HAVE DECLARED [JPS:  I ACKNOWLEDGE] THIS DAY.  I have declared to everyone with this act of mine, as with (II Sam. 19:7), "For you have made clear today that the officers and servicemen mean nothing to you."  
(3) THAT I HAVE ENTERED THE LAND. That I have come from another land to this land.  
(4) THAT ADONAI SWORE TO OUR FATHERS TO ASSIGN US.  As it says (Gen. 17:8), "I have given to you and your offspring to come."  Therefore, I, the stranger, who have come to the land as a resident by way of [Divine] gift, have brought the first fruits to the One who gave the land as a gift or as a sharecropping arrangement.


Verse 4

HE SHALL SET IT DOWN IN FRONT OF THE ALTAR OF ADONAI YOUR GOD.  To show and to proclaim that the first fruits were not brought to the priest, but were brought to God, may He be blessed, and He gave them to the priest with the other gifts to the priests.

Chapter 32



Verse 1



Verse 2

(1) MAY MY DISCOURSE COME DOWN AS THE RAIN.  My Torah will come down and come in a flood like rain to the discerning ones who are prepared to receive the expression of the source of wisdom.  
(2) MY SPEECH DISTILL AS THE DEW.  And provides, according to what is revealed in it, some awareness to the common ones, which, though it be small, it is very good like dew; such that it [the Torah] is LIKE SHOWERS ON VERDURE [JPS: YOUNG GROWTH], that the enlightened ones will perceive wonders in it, ...
(3) AND LIKE DROPLETS ON THE GRASS.  For the common ones also will acquire some knowledge to recognize their Creator in some way.  If so, you, Israel, who have accepted it [the Torah], [I now say:]--

Verse 3

(1) FOR THE NAME OF ADONAI I PROCLAIM.   Here, the one who calls the name of Adonai is the one who prays, as it is said (Lam. 3:55), "I have called on Your name, O LORD, From the depths of the Pit," and similarly (Ps. 99:6), "and Shmu'el among those who call on His name— when they called to Adonai, He answered them."  He [Moshe] said, "If so, then when I pray for the ingathering of exiles, when I say (v.11), 'Like an eagle who rouses its nestlings, [Gliding down to its young, So did [God] spread wings and take them, Bear them along on pinions],' and for the coming of the Messiah, when I say (v.12), 'Adonai alone did guide them,' and at the end of the poem, when I say (v.43), 'O nations, acclaim God’s people,' you, who know the intellectual wonders made clear in His Torah, -- 
(2) GIVE GLORY TO OUR GOD.  Do not attribute to Him mutability regarding what is recounted in the poem about Israel's future and that He would become like an enemy to you, for you know, through the wonders in His Torah, that He is --"

Verse 4

(1) THE ROCK.  Unchanging.  His hand is not too short to save, His ear not too dull to hear.  (See Isa. 59:1). And also, do not attribute to Him any injustice, since you know through wonders that HIS WORK IS PERFECT in actuality -- nothing is to be added to it and nothing subtracted.  
(2) And through this it is known that ALL HIS WAYS ARE JUST.  The His ways of goodness and His attribute of justice are all undoubtedly just.  
(3) A FAITHFUL GOD.  Reliable in what He swore to the Patriarchs to do good to their children because of the merits of the ancestors. 
(4) NEVER UNJUST [JPS: FALSE].  In His bringing retribution.  
(5) RIGHTEOUS [JPS: TRUE].  "He loves righteous deeds" (Ps. 11:7), and in His righteousness, He will not forsake His people.  
(6) AND UPRIGHT INDEED.  He will not deprive any person of a reward, and will compensate His enemies [for their good deeds] openly, and therefore, He is patient and beneficent with the nations in this world.

Verse 5

THEY HAVE DESTROYED FOR HIM, HIS NON-CHILDREN, [BY] THEIR BASENESS, THE CROOKED AND PERVERSE GENERATION.  [JPS:  UNWORTHY CHILDREN— THAT CROOKED, PERVERSE GENERATION— THEIR BASENESS HAS PLAYED GOD FALSE].  However, the crooked and perverse generation are not His children in purity; their baseness in connection with the Calf has destroyed for God, may He be exalted, what was intended, as it is said (Exod. 32:7), "for your people have acted basely."  For He in fact had intended to sanctify Israel, and to sanctify His name in His world through them, so that they would be luminaries among humankind, to understand and to teach, as it is said (Exod. 19:5-6), "Indeed, all the earth is Mine, but you shall be to Me a kingdom of priests."  But they destroyed all this through the Calf.

Verse 6

(1) DO YOU THUS REQUITE ADONAI?  Since He had intended to place you above all the nations, is it appropriate that you should requite Him this way, that you should frustrate His intent, that you should desecrate His holy name, and that you should prevent His achieving the end that He had planned in His saying (Gen. 1:25), "Let Us make humanity in Our image, after Our likeness"? 
(2) DULL PEOPLE.  Disgraced, through your owing gratitude, and repaying favors with evil, the opposite of the generous One, Who is honored in His doing good to one who has never done good for Him.  
(3) AND WITLESS.  In practical affairs, to understand the consequences of a matter, and what will be achieved. 
(4) IS NOT THIS THE FATHER WHO ACQUIRED [JPS: CREATED] YOU?  Not a natural father, who gives some measure of existence by chance; but He is an intentional father, Who gave you existence so that you would be His possession, prepared [for Him] to achieve His desire through you [and to make you] His treasure.  And this is because HE FASHIONED YOU into a nation, for you had not been a nation of any account at all.  
(5) AND PREPARED YOU [JPS: MADE YOU ENDURE].  He gave you preparations so that you would be prepared to be a treasured nation to Him.

Chapter 33



Verse 1

THIS IS THE BLESSING WITH WHICH MOSES [BLESSED ISRAEL (JPS: BADE THE ISRAELITES FAREWELL)].  When God, may He be blessed, showed him the whole Land before his death, so that he would bless the Land, and Israel in it, as had been his intent, when he said (Deut. 3:25), "Let me cross over and let me see," and as had been God's intent, may He be blessed, when He said to him (Num. 27:12), "and view the Land," -- this is the blessing that he blessed.

Verse 2

(1) HE SAID.  Before he began the blessing, which begins (v.25), "May your doorbolts be iron and copper," he spoke this introduction, and prayed for the tribes, that his blessing might rest [on them], for certainly, the words of the blessing everywhere speak with the one who is blessed, as He said (Num. 6:23), "Speak to them," and so all of his words, from "iron and copper" to (v.29), "you shall tread on their backs," were with Israel, but the prior words were with God, requesting with respect to His people.  
(2) ADONAI.  An expression of calling.  
(3) CAME FROM SINAI, AND SHONE ... LIGHTNING FLASHING AT THEM.  He mentions Israel's merits, so that his prayer for them would be accepted and his blessing would rest [on them], and he said to God, may He be blessed, "Hey, there came from Sinai, and shone, and appeared from various places to Israel the holy of flashing lightning, it being the inquiring part that comes from the multitudinous holy, from the right-hand side of the holy, for He indeed gave it transparently.  

Verse 3

(1) EVEN THOUGH [JPS: INDEED] [YOU ARE] A LOVER OF NATIONS [JPS: OF THE PEOPLE].  Even though You are a lover of nations, as You said (Exod. 19:5),, "you shall be My treasured possession more than [JPS: among] all the peoples," and in this way, You have made known that all of humankind is treasured by You, as they said (Avot 3:18), "The human is dear in that he was created in the image."  Nevertheless, ALL OF ITS [JPS: THEIR] HOLY ONES ARE IN YOUR HAND.  Here, You have said that all the ones sanctified by the holy of the fire-law are in Your hand like a bundle of coins, for they are dearer than the rest of the species, as He said (Exod. 19:6), "you shall be to Me a kingdom of priests and a holy nation," as they said (Avot 3:18), "Dear is Israel, in that they are called the children of the Omnipresent.  
(2) THEY FOLLOWED.  They were broken, as (Prov. 29:13) "and an oppressor" [NJPS: "a fraudulent man"].  They prayed with a broken spirit. 
(3) YOUR FEET [JPS: STEPS].  To your footstool on Sinai.  

Verse 4

(1) [v.23]  HE WILL LIFT UP YOUR STATEMENTS, THE TORAH THAT MOSHE COMMANDED US. [JPS: ACCEPTING YOUR PRONOUNCEMENTS.  MOSHE CHARGED US WITH THE TORAH.]  They said to God, may He be blessed, "Let Moshe bring us Your statements, which are the Torah that he commanded us, saying your statements, as it says (Num. 7:89). "he heard the voice speaking with him." 
(2) INHERITANCE OF THE CONGREGATION OF YA'AKOV.  [And] which is for an inheritance for the congregation of Ya'akov, accepted for us and as an inheritance for our children.  

Verse 5

(1) AND IT HAPPENED THAT THE KING OF YESHURUN.  And then, the One who was King in Yeshurun, i.e., God, may He be blessed, Who was King for the inquiring ones and those who grasped the Torah, 
(2) UPON THE GATHERING OF THE HEADS OF THE PEOPLE. At the Giving of the Torah, as he said (Deut. 5:20), "you came up to me, all your tribal heads and elders," and he said (5:21), "[and you said,] 'Adonai our God has just shown us His presence, etc.'"  
(3) THE TRIBES OF ISRAEL TOGETHER.  He was King over all the tribes of Israel together, for then they acknowledged and accepted His kingship, as they said (5:24), "then you tell us everything that Adonai our God tells you, and we will willingly do it."

Verse 6

(1)  MAY REUVEN LIVE.  Even though Reuven selected Transjordan, which is not part of God's land, as he [Yehoshua] said (Josh. 22:19), "If it is because the land of your holding is impure," and accordingly, it is not so fit for attaining through it eternal life, may he nevertheless thrive alone forever.  
(2) AND NOT DIE, THOUGH FEW BE HIS NUMBERS.  And not die in this world, in the manner that his population be few, as in (Gen. 34:30), "my fighters are few in number," even though it is written (Deut. 11:21), "to the end that you may endure in the land."

Verse 7

(1) AND THIS OF JUDAH.  And this request I also request and pray for the land of Yehudah, within which the allotment of Shimon is scattered, so that they will not die in war, for they would go out to war together.  
(2) HE SAID, HEAR, ADONAI, THE VOICE OF JUDAH.  In addition to what he said above -- "This of Yehudah" -- he also said, "Hear, Adonai, the voice of Yehudah" when he prays in war or outside of it.  
(3) THOUGH HIS OWN HANDS STRIVE FOR HIM,  HELP HIM AGAINST HIS FOES. You [God] be "his hands," fighting for him, and also be striving on his behalf and taking vengeance for him, and a help against his foes when they wage war against him.
