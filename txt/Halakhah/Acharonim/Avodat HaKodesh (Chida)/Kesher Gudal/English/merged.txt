Kesher Gudal



Siman 1

As soon as you wake up, say "Mode Ani Lefanecha melech rahum..." and you do not need to wash your hands, and some say you do need to.
One must immediately wash his hands, even if he is not getting up. One should not have a Goy or Jew wash his hands, because they probably didn't wash there hands.
One should dress properly and awake and midnight, and defeat his bad inclination
One may not walk four Amot (cubits) without washed hands, this is a very strict matter. If he has no cup near him, he should walk less then four cub
 It is is forbidden to recite blessings (or learn Torah) in front of the water used to do Netilat Yadayim, one should pour it out or cover it.
...(After Asher Yatzar) Right away recite all the morning blessings until "VaAni Abarchem" (AriZal)
(I have said that "Right away recite all the blessings...") And this is after he is already dressed properly, (Because) one should not dress and bless (simultaneously), because one may not do other things while reciting blessings. 
 In the night, one should say Vidui (The repentance prayer) and the Tiqqun Hazot (The midnight rite) of the Ari Za"l (at midnight) - and the time of midnight is 12 hours after midday (See Birke Yosef, Siman Alef).
(After Tiqqun Hazot,) if one is not able to stay awake until the light of the day, he should sleep, and should not remove his clothing - in order not to enter a doubt to recite the Birkot HaShahar again (See Birke Yosef, Siman Mem Zayin).
There is an obligation for everyone in every place to arise before sunrise (See Mahaziq Beracha, Siman Alef
One is able to drink coffee without sugar before sunrise, and even after sunrise before prayer. (See Birke Yosef, Siman Pe Tet)
