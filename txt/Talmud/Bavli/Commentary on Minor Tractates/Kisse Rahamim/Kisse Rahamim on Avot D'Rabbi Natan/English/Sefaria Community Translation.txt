Halakhah 2

"And they would use silver vessels, etc." — Referring to Tzadok, Boethus, and their students. But in the book Binyan Yehoshua, it says this refers to Antigonus and his students — this seems to be an incidental slip.
"And two breaches emerged from them" — Rambam explains this well in his commentary on the Mishnah. See also Tzemach David, Part 1, Section 3, on this topic.
