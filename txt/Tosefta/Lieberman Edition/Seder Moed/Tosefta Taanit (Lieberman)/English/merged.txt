Chapter 2





Once they declared a fast on Ḥanukkah in Lod. They told R. Liezer, who told R. Yehoshua, and he washed. R. Yehoshua said to them, "Go and fast for having fasted." As long as Rabban Gamliel was standing, the halakha followed his words. Upon Rabban Gamliel's death R. Yehoshua sought to nullify his words. R. Yoḥanan ben Nuri stood on his feet and said, "The way I see it, after the head follows the body. As long as Rabban Gamliel was standing, the halakha followed his words, but now that he's died you want to nullify his words?" R. Yehoshua said, "We hear you." The halakha was set per the words of Rabban Gamliel, and no man contested his words.
