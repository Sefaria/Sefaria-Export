Chapter 3







... Rabbi 'Lazar (=El`azar) the son of Rabbi Zadok says, Father used to pray a short prayer on Sabbath eve:  "Because of Your love, O Lord our God, with which You have loved Your people Israel, and because of Your kindness, Our King, which You have shown to the people of Your covenant, You have granted us, O Lord, this great and holy seventh day in love."


Days without a Musaf service, such as Ḥanukkah and Purim — for Shaḥarit and Minḥa recite the normative eighteen blessings, and say something about the occasion. If one did not say it, one does not return to it. But all days with a Musaf service, such as Rosh Hodesh and Ḥol haMo'ed — for Shaḥarit and Minḥa recite the normative eighteen blessings, and say the daily sanctification in the 'Avodah prayer. R. Liezer says, in the thanksgiving prayer. If one did not say it, one <i>does</i> return to it. For Musaf, say seven blessings, and say the daily sanctification in the middle.
