Chapter 3


Rabbi Chalafta ben Kavina says, Garlic from Balbeck is impure because they irrigate it with water, and afterwards they hang it. The Sages said, if so, it would be impure to Chalafta ben Kavina and pure to all [the rest] of Israel. Yehoshua ben Perachya says, [with regards to] wheat that is imported from Alexandria, they rendered it impure on account of [the wheat being sprinkled with] their baling machine (<i>antliyah</i>). The Sages said, if so, it would be impure to Yehoshua ben Perachya and pure to all [the rest] of Israel.
