Chapter 10


How are the repairs of the soul, reincarnation, and transmigration: The service of the soul is the 613 commandments. If it completes them, it goes to its rest. But, if not, it returns and is reincarnated. Not all of it is reincarnated, but rather the parts that need repair.
What is reincarnation [<i>gilgul</i>], and what is transmigration [<i>eibur</i>]? Reincarnation is from the time of birth until death; the arrival of [the soul with] transmigration is at any time, and its departure is at any time. A commandment that it was obligated about, it completes with reincarnation; a commandment it was not obligated about, it completes with transmigration. When it completes them, it leaves. The righteous may reincarnate thousands [of times], the evil up to four, as it is stated, <i>for four, I will not return it</i> (Amos 2:4).
