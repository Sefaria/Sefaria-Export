Torah Learning




























Through Torah and prayer, one merits joy. Through joy, one merits holy boldness. Through holy boldness, one merits drawing close to true tzaddikim (righteous individuals). Through them, one merits perfect faith. Through faith, one attains the aspect of "hands"—the ability to accept reproof from true rebukers. This, in turn, saves one from exile and conflict, increasing peace in the world and establishing peace between Israel and their Father in Heaven. At that point, one rectifies and seals holiness with the concept of a "seal within a seal." (Likutei Moharan I, Torah 22:9)
