The commandment of procreation

The matter of the commandment of being fruitful and multiplying -  See, our sages say (Niddah 31) “There are three partners in a person: the father provides seed and from him comes the whiteness – bones, sinews, nails, and the brain in his head, and the whiteness of the eye. The mother gives “seed” from which [comes] the redness - skin, flesh, etc… and The Holy One Blessed be He gives him ruach-spirit and neshama-soul.” Now, it is known based on that which is written on the verse (Yeshaya 57:16) “and the souls I made” that a person has two souls: an animalistic soul that is enclothed within the blood of a person to give it life, and also it gives a person intelligence to comprehend all manner of wisdom in the world, and it draws [it’s spiritual root] from above from the spiritual forces, which is the quality of the “face of the ox” on the Godly Chariot, and from the “face of a man” [on the Chariot] is drawn the animalistic souls of the Jews, and the second soul within Jews is a actual part of God, as it’s written “God is a portion of His people,” because it is a spark of actual Godliness and it is drawn from the quality of “the likeness of a man upon the throne,” which is the likeness of the form of the glory of Hashem. (see Ezekiel 1:26, 28)                  And this is [the meaning of] that which is written (Genesis 9:6) “in the image of God He made Man.” Because the essential quality of the ten sefiros (emanations) of zeer anpin correspond to the form of a human structure by way of analogy, and like that which is written also the neshama-soul which is drawn from it is in the form of a human with 248 spiritual “limbs” and 365 spiritual “sinews,” and like that which is written in the Etz Chaim that all the souls put together are like one human enclothed in the human [structure/form] of the sefiros, and the godly soul is enclothed in the animalistic soul, which is also according to the form of a human with 248 limbs and 365 sinews, and is enclothed in the actual physical limbs of a person, which are actually aligned [with the soul’s structure] in form and likeness and the structure of the “limbs” and “sinews” of the spiritual soul, as is found in the Zohar (I 38a) – “the lower world is parallel the upper world.”           And now, to understand all this, and the matter of the effects [resulting from] the time of a person's cohabitation in drawing down a soul, it is necessary to preface first the matter of the descent of the soul to this world, why it [is necessary]. Since it is known that the soul, before its descent into the body stands on a very lofty level and derives pleasure from the radiance of the Shechina (Divine Presence of Immanence), as it's written regarding Elijah, "As Hashem lives, before whom I stood" (Kings I 17:1), according to its place and level in Atzilus, Briyah, Yetzira, or Asiyah, and why it descends multitudes [of levels] into a physical body that conceals the light of holiness of Hashem, which it is drawn from, and [the body] runs after physical pleasures of this world by its nature, in that they are its foundation and nature and cause the concealment of godliness. And also, since in truth, all the pleasures and comforts of this world are as nothing, even if one was able to live a thousand years in peace and tranquility and security and be a king of kings, such that you didn't lack anything in the world of all its pleasures, it is still impossible, in truth, to compare them with one hour of the spiritual pleasure, even the lowest of the low levels in the spiritual worlds. For that which is written, (Avos 4:17) "Better is one hour of spiritual satisfaction in the World to Come than all the life of this world." And how much more so [the spiritual pleasure of] the upper Garden of Eden, which cannot be compared at all to the lower Garden of Eden, so much so that when a soul ascends from the lower garden to the upper garden, it needs to "immerse" in the River Dinor to "forget" all the pleasures of the lower Garden of Eden so that it will not be confused in the upper garden - the same way that a soul ascending from this world to the lower garden needs at first to "forget" its {vision?} (translation not certain) of this world, as is known in the Zohar, since it is actually like a foreign thought that confuses...etc.       If so, any understanding person can grasp the matter to picture the awesome power of the pleasures of the upper garden, and there are many more levels above and above that that are not even mentioned in books, only these two levels [are], but in truth, it is known to us from the holy authors to which the paths of heaven are revealed to them, that there are levels ad infinitum, and each one is greater than the the essence of the one below it, like that which is written by our sages (Chagigah 13a) "the feet of the chayos [angels] are corresponding to all." And to this our sages had in mind in there statement (Brachos 64a) "Torah scholars have no rest in the World to Come" - there go from strength to strength according to the concept of ascending to higher and higher worlds to take pleasure in Hashem:                         Furthermore, the magnitude of the soul's descent can be understood - how it goes from "a high roof to a low pit" - through another matte, namely, that which our sages stated (Chagigah 15b) [about"acher" (the heretic Elisha ben Avuyah)] that "better he be judged properly [by the heavenly court] and then go into the World to Come" - this means to say that even the lower Garden of Eden. Now the judgement and suffering of Gehenom are very great and strong, as its written in Iggeres Hateshuva (chapter 12) in the name of the Ramban, that even the suffering of Job for seventy years is not comparable to even one hour in Gehenom, and the judgement of the wicked in Gehenom is twelve months (Eiduyos 2:10) and they are more than eight, closer to nine thousand hours, and from the time that Acher died in the lifetime of R' Meir (who was before R' Simon b' Yochai as is written in the Rambam's introduction to the Mishnah and in the [Tosfos Yom tov?] in the first chapter of Shevuos (mishnah 4) that he was the teacher of Rebbe who was the teacher of R' Yochanan, who lived a long life and when he died, the smoke stopped ascended from Acher's grave [a sign that his time in Gehenom had ceased]. And all this is relevant to the lower Garden of Eden, how much more so many multitudes and multitudes more is the level of the lower Garden of Eden of which it is worth to bear much suffering just for it alone, something that isn't true for the pleasures of this world [that one wouldn't] bear [even] minor suffering to earn it  ...  ויאמרו לו הן וכו' [translation uncertain] ...  Rather, in truth there is no comparison between the pleasure of the body to the spiritual pleasures and even between the pleasures of the lower Garden of Eden, and how much more so the spiritual worlds of which the lower Garden of Eden compared to the higher Garden of Eden is like the difference between physical pleasures and the spiritual pleasured of the lower Garden of Eden, so to in this was higher and higher the levels go with no end, so that when an understanding person puts his heart to contemplate this matter he'll be shocked into silence by the wondrous and extremely lofty levels that the soul had to descend from down into this world of physicality. 
... And the explanation of these matters is understood according to what our Sages say (Menachos 29b) "'In Yah, the Lord, is the Rock of eternity' (Yeshayahu 26:4) - with [the letter] Yud the World to Come was created and with [the letter] Hey the present world was created." The explanation: It is known that the Ein Sof surrounds all worlds and fills all worlds. Meaning, that He gives life to the worlds through two ways. One is through the enlightenment [of his light/presence] becoming enclothed within the inner and essential aspect of every single thing according to each thing's capacity to contain [the light] and its existence not be nullified. This enlightenment is called filling [all worlds], and that is what our Sages reference in their statement: (midrash Teilim on "barchi Nafshi") "Just as the soul fills the body, likewise Hashem fills the whole world entirely." And just as the light and revelation of the life-force of the nefesh-soul in the body has many levels, and the essential resting place and truest revelation of it is in the head, and then the heart, the rest of the inner limbs, and after that the hands and feet until it reaches even the hair and fingernails, each one in descending order from the previous in the level of quality and quantity of the nefesh-soul's revelation of life-force, this is likewise true in general for all worlds, in that they are [created in] the likeness of the Form (partzuf) of the Man {by way of analogy?} and the light of Hashem illuminates and is enclothed in their inner essence to give them life, and anyone who draws closer to His light receives more and more enlightenment and revelations, and [descending levels] after it is less and less [light] from level to level, in "clothes" and many concealments according to the nature of the vessel in what it can receive and contain. And within the whole process of these enlightenments are all the worlds of ABIYA (atzilus-Emanation, beriyah-Creation, yetzirah-Formation, asiyah-Making) as is known, that Abba-Father "nests" in Atzilus, Imma-Mother in Beriyah-Creation, and Tiferes itself in Yetzirah-Formation, and so on, and the  many level described previously in the manner of ascending spiritual levels, on after the other, high and higher, as mentioned earlier in the matter of the Garden of Eden, they are are all from this enlightenment that is filling all worlds and that enclothes itself in the many levels that have no end, since the end of all the levels in the spiritual worlds is the concept of the lower garden of Eden, as mentioned previously, and everything that is above that draws closer to the source, where there is revelation of the illumination of G-dliness without constrictions or many filters, and there will be greater delight with greater strength because the delight comes from the revelation of G-dliness,  by way of analogy down below  [in the lower worlds] that with intellectual understanding comes delight/pleasure necessarily, for that is also the way it is on high, according to the wisdom and understanding the delights from the Ancient of Ancients (i.e. Keser-Crown) is drawn, as is written "for with you is the source of life (Tehillim 36:10)". And if you will ask about these myriad of levels [how that fits with idea of them] all being included in general within the Form of the supernal Man, know that the general is split into particulars, and also the particulars is a general principle for more particulars after it in many many levels, one after the other in the manner of general principles, [followed by] specifics, [then] a general principle. Because all that we said about the the principles that are set up through all worlds, each one {world?} has numerous worlds and each one of those divides into many Partzufim-Faces/Forms, in that in every partzuf there is the essential aspect of the [supernal] Man, within which is contained the many thousands of particular worlds without end, where each one is greater than the one after it as mentioned previously and as discussed... on the verse "great is our God and abundant in strength (Tehillim 147:5) ...  {translation unknown until אך}                 However, all [these worlds] are in general part of the concept of [the Supernal] Man and the light of Hashem that sustains them and gives life to them is called "filling all worlds," like a soul to the body. And this is what [is meant by what is] written by our Sages that "with the [letter] Yud the World to Come was created, since the concept of chochma-Wisdom is called (linked to) the Yud, as is known, and the idea is that chochma-Wisdom is the beginning of the chain of development/evolution (hishtalshelus) [of all creation/worlds] as it is written (Tehillim 110:10) "the beginning [is] wisdom," and the enclothement of the light of the Ain Sof in [the worlds of] ABIYA is through chochma-Wisdom, as is written in likkutai amarim (chapter 2) [in reference to] (Tehillim 104:24) "you made everything with wisdom." Now from chochma-Wisdom and downward is the idea of the chain of evolution/development of all words but chochma-Wisdom itself 'comes from nothingness' - not in the manner of the development/evolution of all words because the light of the Ain Sof is loftier a thousand levels and beyond any measure than [even] the upper chochma-Wisdom, and as it's known from the explanation of the Maamar (lesson) ...  {translation unknown until  שעצמות}            that the essence of the light of Ain Sof is completely simple and undifferentiated, and there are no divisions into the ten sefiros, chochma-wisdom, binah-understanding, etc. at all, as is written in likutay amarim, and for this [light of Ain Sof] to take on the characteristic of a seemingly independently existing creation is the idea of chochma-Wisdom, which comes about through a great and intense constriction [of the light of Ain Sof], that is [the idea of] what is written (Iyov 28:12) [wisdom] "comes from nothingness," since the generation of chochma-Wisdom from the light of the Ain Sof is through a "jumping" of levels, like the idea of  "something from nothing," and not like the idea of the chain of development/evolution of worlds, through cause and effect, something from something, for if it were not this way, chochma-Wisdom could never come about from the light of the Ain Sof, since it is totally simple and unable to be defined as something with limitations and boundaries such as Chochma-Wisdom, for it is a specific and unique power. Therefore, Chochma-Wisdom is called (linked to) the Yud, since it comes about from such a great constriction of the light of the Ain Sof it is but a small dot of illumination from that original light, by way of analogy the way the [letter] Yud [is just a small dot]. And from chochma-wisdom and onwards this illumination stretches on in the chain of development/evolution [through] cause and effect, and this is the concept of filling all worlds. Therefore, our Sages state that the World to Come was created with the Yud because all the pleasures of the should in the Garden of Eden in all the many levels reaching higher and higher from there, their source and root is the concept of chochma-wisdom, as is known from the matter regarding (Breishis 2:10) "and a river went forth from Eden..." However, the essence of the light of Ain Sof is so much loftier than this, even chochma-wisdom which is the beginning of all the development/evolution [of worlds] and is called Yud, it is nullified completely like a drop in the {ocean?}, and more than that,
