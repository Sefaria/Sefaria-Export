Beshalach



Chapter 1

“See, for Hashem has given you the Shabbat…”  In Midrash Tehillim (section 92)  What does “See” mean?  Rabbi Yitzchak said: Every aspect of Shabbat is doubled.  The omer is doubled,  Two omers for one person,  Its offering is doubled,  And on the day of Shabbat, two lambs, etc. Its commandments are doubled – “Remember” and “Keep”, etc. And in Shemot Rabbah, Parashat Beshalach, end of section 25, That the Shabbat is equal to all the commandments, etc. – see there.
